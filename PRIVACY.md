# Privacy

Fast-Tabber keeps tab and bookmark details, cached page text, requested screenshots, saved collections, and undo history on your computer. It reads the pages selected for indexing or inspection without following their links. Cached text is reused until you ask for a refresh.

Your connected agent receives the information it requests, such as page titles, URLs, text, accessibility information, links, or screenshots. If you use a cloud-connected agent, its model provider may receive that information. An agent running a local model can keep inference on your device, depending on its other integrations. Fast-Tabber does not request cookies, credentials, form values, or incognito content.

The connection between Chrome and Fast-Tabber stays on your computer and requires pairing. Setup explains browser permissions and privacy. Bookmark access is optional and requires your approval in Chrome.

Ask your agent to clear cached data when you want to remove saved page text and screenshots. You will be asked to confirm removal. Saved collections and undo history are kept unless you explicitly choose to remove all local data.
